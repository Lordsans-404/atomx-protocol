// HOW IT WORKS

Commit. Prove. Earn.
Or Pay the Price.

A simple loop backed by behavioral science, smart contracts, and AI — that makes breaking your habits genuinely costly and keeping them genuinely rewarding.

01
Define Your Commitment ON-CHAIN
Set a habit (reading, exercise, coding), duration in days, daily target in minutes, and stake an amount of USDT. Your stake is locked in an escrow vault — publicly verifiable on Solana.
02
Execute Daily & Submit Proof AI VERIFIED
Complete your habit, hit the in-app timer, and upload a photo. LLaMA 4 Scout analyzes your image against your commitment context — confidence score ≥30 = valid proof, submitted on-chain as a ProofRecord PDA.
03
Earn Daily Validator NFTs cNFT
Every verified day mints a compressed NFT badge on Solana via Crossmint. Your on-chain habit history becomes a verifiable identity — a permanent track record of discipline.
04
Claim Rewards + Champion Medal
Complete all days → claim your full stake back, plus a loyalty bonus (1–3% from the global rewards pool), and a unique Champion Medal NFT. The more commitments you complete, the higher your bonus tier.
05
Miss a Day? Slash Happens.
Miss your deadline: short commitments (≤7 days) get 100% slashed. Longer ones get 40% on first failure, 100% on second. Slashed funds are distributed — not burned — into the ecosystem pools.

ANTI-PLAGIARISM
Every proof image is SHA-256 hashed and stored on-chain. Reusing a photo is mathematically impossible.
SEQUENTIAL VALIDATION
Smart contract enforces sequential day proofs. Day 3 can only be submitted after Day 2 — no skipping.

Smart contract rejects proofs under 50% of daily target minutes. No gaming the timer — it's enforced in Rust.
